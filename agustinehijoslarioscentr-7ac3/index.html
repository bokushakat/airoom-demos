All 123 reviews come from Treatwell, the listing for the salon at Av. de la Aurora 25, inside Centro Comercial Larios. We read 26 of its 52 pages, from the most recent ones to older ones. Only reviews with text are counted.

We could not read Google Maps reviews (Google blocks automated reading), and our searches did not turn up reviews of this salon on other platforms. Critical reviews are few: 11 of the 123 contain any complaint at all. Treat the patterns below as early signals, not a verdict.

Top 3 recurring complaints

01

The final bill is higher than the customer expected

Process: pricing and quotes
El precio se encareció al estimado en el correo. (The price went up from the estimate in the email.) David, Treatwell · source
100 euros creo que es muy caro, en otros sitios cobran un 40% menos con igual resultado (I think 100 euros is very expensive; other places charge 40% less for the same result.) Nacho, Treatwell · source
Los precios me parecen caros para el servicio que dan. (The prices seem expensive for the service they give.) Encarni, Treatwell · source

Short versions of the same point appear too: «Demasiado caro» (too expensive) and «es un poco caro» (it is a bit expensive).

What to fix: the problem is less the price itself than the surprise. When the total will go above the online estimate (longer hair, extra product, an added service), say the new figure in the chair, before the work starts.
02

What was booked is not what happens

Process: booking and handover to the chair
Lo que no me gusta y ya me ha ocurrido dos veces, es pedir cita con un profesional y cuando llegas te asignan otro. (What I don't like, and it has happened to me twice, is booking with one stylist and being given another when you arrive.) Raquel, Treatwell · source
Saque cita SOLO CORTE Y LAVADO y me sentí obligada a secarme el pelo cuando yo solo quería cortar y lavar (I booked ONLY A CUT AND WASH and felt obliged to have my hair dried when I only wanted a cut and wash.) Amparo J, Treatwell · source

Amparo adds that she was charged the same as a full blow-dry: «NO LO VEO RAZONABLE» (I don't think it's reasonable).

What to fix: if the booked stylist is not available, tell the customer before the visit and let them choose. In the chair, repeat back what was booked, and ask before adding any step that changes the price.
03

Reception and small hospitality details

Process: front desk and comfort during long services
tan solo puedo poner una queja la recepcionista no le daría un 10 creo q por su forma de tratar a la.clientela le daría un 5 como muchísimo. (I can make only one complaint: I wouldn't give the receptionist a 10. For the way she treats customers I'd give her a 5 at most.) Montserrat, Treatwell · source
Eche de menos que no me ofrecieran un agua o cafe ya que el tratamiento duró casi dos horas (I missed being offered water or coffee, as the treatment lasted almost two hours.) Pilar, Treatwell · source

Both customers rated the stylists highly. The complaint is about the moments around the service. Your Treatwell listing says customers are offered a drink, so this is about doing it every time.

What to fix: make the welcome and the drink a fixed step for any service over an hour, not left to whoever is free. The front desk is also where bills are paid, so it shapes how a price surprise (complaint 1) feels.

Two one-off results complaints also appear: an eyelash tint that stained the skin under the eyes (source) and a child's cut described as «muy básicos» (very basic) (source). Neither repeats, so they are not in the top 3.

What to fix first

This week: a 30-second check at the start of every appointment. The stylist confirms three things out loud: the service booked, who is doing it, and the expected total. If anything will be added or the price will change, they ask first.

This one habit addresses complaints 1 and 2 together, costs nothing, and needs only a short team briefing.

  • Named stylists customers return for. Toñi, Teresa, Noelia, Ricardo, Lourdes, Luisa, Mari Carmen and others are mentioned by name again and again. «Viajo desde Zaragoza para ver a mi familia y a Pedro.» (I travel from Zaragoza to see my family and Pedro.) source

  • Good products, and customers notice. «Se nota que utilizan buenos productos y que los peluqueros saben lo que hacen.» (You can tell they use good products and the stylists know what they're doing.) source
  • Punctuality and easy booking. «la cita comenzó puntual cosa que se valora mucho» (the appointment started on time, which is much appreciated) source
